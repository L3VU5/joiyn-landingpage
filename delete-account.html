Delete Your Account

How to delete your Joiyn account and what happens to your data.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once you confirm, your profile, the events you host and your personal data are removed and can't be recovered.

Delete from within the app

The quickest way is in the Joiyn app:

  1. Open the Joiyn app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm when the app asks.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo, date of birth, gender, about me, interests, languages and city
  • Your sign-in: email address, password, and the link to Google or Apple if you used them
  • Events you host, with their participant lists, chat messages and photos
  • Your requests and places in other people's events
  • Your notifications, notification settings, saved searches and push token
  • Your stay-in-touch choices and connections, your blocks, and content you hid by reporting it
  • Feedback and crash reports you sent from the app

In events hosted by other people, your chat messages are replaced with "[deleted]" and the photos you posted there are removed. Where other people's notifications mention you, your name is replaced with "Deleted user".

What stays

  • Reports. Reports you sent and reports about you are kept for safety reasons, including the copy emailed to our support inbox. They hold user IDs (not names or email addresses), the reason and any details the reporter wrote.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Automatic crash reports in Sentry aren't tagged with your account and are deleted when Sentry's retention period ends.
  • Emails you sent to support@joiyn.app stay in our mailbox.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app or your account, you can request deletion by email. Send a message to support@joiyn.app from the email address linked to your Joiyn account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, visit our Support page or read our Privacy Policy.
