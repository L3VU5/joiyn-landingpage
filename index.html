Features

Everything you need to meet people locally

Browse events, chat with the group before you arrive, and stay in touch with the people you click with. If something feels off, you can report it or block the person.

Find events near you

See what's happening around your city, filtered by what you're into and how far you want to go. Nothing there yet? Tap "Notify me about new events" and we'll let you know when one shows up.

Pick what you're into

Running, board games, bouldering, padel, coffee. Choose your activities and Joiyn shows you events that match.

Host your own

Set up an event in a minute: place, time, group size and a few words from you. You decide who joins.

Chat before you meet

Get to know the group beforehand, so showing up feels easy instead of awkward. Send a GIF straight from the chat, powered by Klipy. Press and hold a message to react with an emoji.

Stay in touch

After the event, pick the people you'd like to see again. If they pick you too, you can invite each other to your next events. See how many events someone has hosted and attended, and where you've met before.

Bring your friends

Share an event on WhatsApp or by text. Friends who have Joiyn go straight to it in the app. Everyone else sees the event page with its invitation code.

How it works

Three steps to your next meetup

Pick your interests, find something happening nearby, and show up. That's pretty much it.

Build your profile

Sign up with your email, Google or Apple, then pick your interests and your city. It takes about two minutes.

Find something you like

Browse events in your city and ask to join. If an event is full, join the waiting list. Once the host approves you, you're in the group chat.

Show up & connect

Meet the people, have a good time and stay in touch with whoever you click with.
