Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joiyn ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Joiyn mobile application and website. Please read it carefully before using our services.

1. Who We Are

Joiyn is a social event matching platform that connects people through shared interests and location-based event discovery. By using Joiyn, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at: support@joiyn.app

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to run Joiyn:

Account & Profile Data

  • Email address (required for registration)
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from that provider to create your account
  • Date of birth (used to check the minimum age. You choose whether your age is visible on your profile)
  • User ID (generated by our authentication service)
  • Display name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Gender
  • "About me" text and the languages you speak, if you add them
  • Selected interests and activities
  • Your city
  • The app language you use, so notifications reach you in that language

Location Data

  • The city or region you search in and your profile city
  • Your device's location when you choose to search near you. It is sent with the search and not stored on our servers. One exception in app version 1.0.1: if the app reports an error to Sentry (see Device & Technical Data), the report can include the addresses of recent requests, and for a nearby search these contain the coordinates. Later versions remove them.
  • The location of events you create (the place name and the map pin)
  • The search radius you pick, sent with each search

We collect location data only to provide core app functionality (finding and creating events near you). We do not track your location in the background or sell location data to third parties.

User-Generated Content

  • Events you create, including the name, notes, place, date and time, group size, costs and cover photo
  • Chat messages and photos you send in event chats
  • From the next app update: GIFs you send in chat (found through Klipy) and emoji reactions to messages
  • Reports you send: what you reported, the reason and any details you add
  • Feedback you send from Settings, with an optional rating
  • Crash reports you send from the app's error screen. They contain the error details, your platform, the app version and your user ID

App Activity & Usage Data

  • Events you host, ask to join, join or leave, including places on a waiting list
  • Your choices after an event ("Stay in touch" or "Not for me") and the connections they create
  • Invitations you send to or receive from people you're in touch with
  • People you block, and content you hide by reporting it
  • Searches you save with "Notify me about new events" (activity and city)
  • Your in-app notifications and whether you have read them

Device & Technical Data

  • Push notification token and platform (iOS or Android), to send you notifications
  • Your notification settings
  • Automatic crash and error reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry with the error, your device model, operating system and app version, and a short trail of recent app activity (such as taps, network requests and log messages). These reports are not tagged with your account.
  • Server logs: records of requests and errors on our servers, which can include your user ID

Website

When you visit joiyn.app, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and protect the site. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies on the site. It remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage, and its fonts and icons load from joiyn.app itself.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To create and secure your account using our identity service (AWS Cognito), including sign-in with Google or Apple if you choose it.
  • App functionality: To show you relevant events, match you with participants who share your interests, and enable you to create and manage events.
  • Event discovery: To surface events based on your city, interests and search area.
  • Messaging: To deliver in-app chat messages between event participants.
  • Notifications: To tell you about join requests, approvals, new messages, event changes, reminders and new events that match your saved searches.
  • Safety & support: To investigate in-app reports (including profiles, events, and images), apply blocks, respond to support requests, and enforce our community guidelines.
  • Fixing problems: To find and fix crashes and errors using crash reports and server logs.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to provide the services you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: Keeping the platform secure, preventing abuse, handling reports, and finding and fixing errors.
  • Consent: Where we ask for your permission (push notifications, location access), you may withdraw it at any time via your device settings.
  • Legal obligation: Where we are required to process data to comply with applicable law.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following circumstances:

Other Users

Signed-in users can open your profile and see your name, photo, age (unless you hide it), gender, about me, interests, languages and city. Your date of birth, country and email address are not shown. From the next app update, your profile also shows how many events you have hosted and attended, and up to three past events you and the viewer both took part in. Someone you block sees less of your profile; our Support page explains how blocking works.

Once a host approves you, your name, photo, age (unless hidden) and gender appear in the event's participant list, and the other participants see your chat messages with your name and photo. The host sees everyone who asks to join.

Your own coordinates are never shared with other users. An event's exact meeting point (the map pin) is visible only to the host and the participants the host has approved. The place name the host enters is visible to everyone.

Public Event Pages

Every open event has a page on joiyn.app that anyone with the link can open without an account, for example when the event is shared on WhatsApp. It shows the event name, activity, date and time, city and place name, the cover photo, the group size and free spots, the cost details, the host's first name, the invitation code, and whether the host added notes. It never shows the map pin, the notes or who is taking part. Search engines may index the page while the event is open. Once the event has ended, the page shows only its name, activity, date and city, and asks search engines not to index it.

Service Providers & Sub-processors

We use the following third-party services to operate Joiyn:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Our backend runs in the eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) region: API Gateway (app requests and the chat connection), Lambda (server code), DynamoDB (database), Cognito (accounts and sign-in), S3 (photos), CloudWatch (server logs) and SES (sign-up and password reset emails, and the emails that forward in-app reports to our support inbox). Photos reach your phone through Amazon CloudFront, a worldwide delivery network.
  • Expo: Mobile build infrastructure and push notification delivery. A notification carries the text you see on screen; for a new chat message that is the first 100 characters of it.
  • Apple Inc.: App distribution via the App Store, Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service (APNs), and the maps shown in the app on iPhone (Apple Maps).
  • Google LLC: App distribution via Google Play, Google sign-in, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), the maps shown in the app on Android (Google Maps), and place search when you set an event's location (Google Places: the text you type and the area shown on the map go from your phone to Google). Our support inbox at support@joiyn.app is a Gmail mailbox: your emails to us and the email copy of each in-app report are stored there.
  • Sentry: Crash and error reports from the app (see Device & Technical Data), stored in Sentry's EU data region.
  • Cloudflare: Hosts this website and the public event pages, and forwards email sent to support@joiyn.app to our inbox.
  • Klipy (from the next app update): GIF search in chat. When you open the GIF picker, the app sends Klipy your search text and a country code based on your app language. When you send a GIF, Klipy is told which GIF and the search that found it. GIFs load from Klipy's servers, so Klipy sees the IP address of everyone in the chat who views them. We don't send Klipy your name, email address or user ID.

Each provider is bound by appropriate data processing agreements and must handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy law.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Joiyn, our users, or the public.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal data while your account exists, with these limits:

  • Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Automatic crash reports are deleted by Sentry when its retention period ends.
  • Events, participant lists, chat messages and photos have no automatic expiry yet. They stay after an event ends and are deleted with the event, for example when its host deletes their account.
  • Notifications are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete your profile, sign-in, the events you host, your notifications and the other data listed on our Account Deletion page. In events hosted by others, your chat messages are replaced with "[deleted]" and your photos are removed.
  • Reports (the ones you send and the ones about you) are kept after account deletion so we can deal with abuse and safety issues. We have not set a fixed expiry for them yet.
  • Emails to support@joiyn.app, including report emails, stay in our mailbox until we delete them.

7. Data Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Our backend infrastructure is hosted on AWS with industry-standard security controls including IAM access policies and encrypted storage. Photos are served through signed links that expire.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data. You can edit most of your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data. You can delete your account directly from within the Joiyn app or via our Account Deletion page.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction: Ask us to restrict processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time via device settings or by contacting us.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@joiyn.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

You must be at least 13 to use Joiyn. The app asks for your date of birth during sign-up and doesn't let anyone younger create a profile. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Joiyn, please contact us and we will delete the account. See also our Child Safety Standards.

10. International Data Transfers

Our backend and database are hosted in the AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany) region, and Sentry stores crash reports in its EU region. Several of our providers are based in the United States: Amazon (AWS), Expo, Apple, Google, Sentry, Cloudflare and Klipy. Photos are delivered through Amazon CloudFront, whose servers are spread around the world. Where personal data is transferred to the United States or another country outside the EEA, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for providers certified under it, or on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

11. Third-Party Links

Joiyn may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

12. Push Notifications

We send push notifications to keep you informed about your events and connections. You can turn each type on or off in the app under Settings → Notifications, or disable all of them in your device's notification settings. Disabling notifications will not affect your ability to use the core features of Joiyn.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you via in-app notification or email (if provided) and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Joiyn after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@joiyn.app
  • Support: Support page
