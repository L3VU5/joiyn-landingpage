2. What Joiyn is

Joiyn is a free mobile app for iOS and Android for finding and hosting small local meetups, which we call events: a run, a board games night, bouldering, a coffee, and so on.

  • You create a profile with your name, photo, date of birth, gender, city, languages, a few words about yourself and your interests.
  • On the Join tab you search for events by activity and area and ask to join. The host approves or declines your request. When an event is full, you can join the waiting list.
  • You can host your own events and decide who gets in.
  • Every event has a group chat for its participants: text, photos, GIFs and emoji reactions.
  • Every open event has a public web page with an invitation code that you can share with friends, and you can add an event to your calendar.
  • After an event, you choose who you would like to stay in touch with.

To use Joiyn you need an iPhone or Android phone with internet access, the app from the App Store or Google Play, and an account (email, or sign in with Google or Apple).

Joiyn only puts people in touch. Events are organised by users, not by us.

  • One account per person, created in your own name and with your real date of birth. Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for everything done through your account.

4. Costs

Joiyn is free. Finding and joining events, hosting your own and the chat cost nothing, and there are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Some hosts list a cost for the activity itself: a court booking, an entrance fee, a shared bill. You settle that directly with the host or the venue, never through Joiyn. We are not a party to that arrangement, we don't handle the money and we can't refund it.

5. Meeting people in person

Joiyn brings together people who don't know each other. We don't verify identities, hosts or venues, and we aren't present at events. You decide whom to meet, at your own risk. A few habits that help:

  • Meet in public places, especially the first time.
  • Tell someone where you are going and with whom.
  • If something feels wrong, leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
  • Report anything that worries you in the app. In an emergency, call the local emergency number (112 in the EU).
